### Known issue

Something specific has already gone wrong, looked off, or felt limiting, and the question is whether it's known, documented, or already fixed — not whether to keep using the technology at all.

Angles, in priority order: `known-issues`, `official-sources`, `community-sentiment`. Three angles, fits the cap in Step 4 exactly — no queue needed.

`known-issues` runs first since it's the direct answer to "is this known." `official-sources` checks for a fix, a changelog entry, or a maintainer acknowledgment. `community-sentiment` shows how widely other people have hit the same thing — a single obscure report reads very differently from a pattern spanning multiple independent threads. `alternatives` stays out of scope here: a known issue isn't automatically a reason to look elsewhere, and that's a separate ask with its own playbook.
