### Compare options

The person is solving a problem by deciding between options — "what else is out there for X", "how do other teams solve this."

Angles, in priority order: `alternatives`, `official-sources`, `known-issues`, `community-sentiment`. Four angles — exceeds the cap of 3 in Step 4. `alternatives`, `official-sources`, and `known-issues` spawn first since they're what the comparison is actually built on; `community-sentiment` queues and spawns once one of the first three returns.

This is the one playbook where the queue in Step 4 always applies. `alternatives` goes first in priority, not last, because it's the actual point of a comparative ask — the other two running alongside it ground each alternative in what's documented and known-broken, and `community-sentiment` rounds it out once a slot opens.
